# Thiet ke dien cuc cho cam bien Velostat tren dem khi

Ngay cap nhat: 2026-07-24

## Ket luan nhanh

Dung la Velostat can dien cuc dan dien ep vao no de do thay doi dien tro khi co ap luc. Tuy nhien khong nen dung tam dong cung day. Nen dung:

- copper tape / copper foil mong co keo dan;
- hoac vai dan dien;
- hoac bang bac dan dien;
- dan tren nen PET/Kapton/vai mong de tao dien cuc mem.

Voi dem khi, khuyen nghi dung patch rieng tung cell, khong trai mot tam Velostat lon lien tuc tren toan bo dem.

## Hai kieu dien cuc phu hop

### Phuong an A - Sandwich electrode, on dinh nhat

Cau truc mot cam bien:

1. Lop vai phu/TPU mong o tren cung.
2. Dien cuc tren bang copper tape hoac vai dan dien.
3. Velostat patch 40-50 mm.
4. Dien cuc duoi bang copper tape hoac vai dan dien.
5. Lop nen PET/Kapton/vai mong.
6. Tui silicone khi.

Dien tro do giua dien cuc tren va dien cuc duoi. Khi bi ep, dien tro giam.

Uu diem:

- Tin hieu on dinh hon.
- Do ap luc theo chieu thang dung tot hon.
- De hieu va de hieu chuan.

Nhuoc diem:

- Moi cell can it nhat 2 tiep diem dien, neu khong dung ma tran hang-cot thi day nhieu.

### Phuong an B - Interdigitated comb electrode, de che tao hon

Day la kieu hai duong dong hinh rang luoc dan xen ke nhau tren cung mot mat phang. Hai mang dong khong cham nhau. Velostat dat len tren de noi dien tro giua hai mang khi co luc ep.

Thong so goi y cho patch 45 x 45 mm:

- Kich thuoc Velostat: 45 x 45 mm.
- Nen PET/Kapton: 50 x 50 mm.
- Be rong ngon dong: 3 mm.
- Khe giua hai ngon dong: 2 mm.
- Chieu dai ngon dong: 30-36 mm.
- Cach mep ngoai toi dong: toi thieu 3 mm.
- Bo tron dau dong hoac dan bang copper tape khong de canh sac.

Uu diem:

- Chi can lam dien cuc tren mot lop.
- De cat bang copper tape.
- It lam cung cum cam bien hon sandwich co dong hai mat.

Nhuoc diem:

- Tin hieu phu thuoc tiep xuc be mat, de bi anh huong boi truot/nghieng.
- Do lap lai kem hon sandwich.
- Can co lop ep/cover de Velostat tiep xuc deu voi cac ngon dong.

## Khuyen nghi cho ban hien tai

Neu muc tieu la de tai nghien cuu va can du lieu ap luc-thoi gian dang tin cay:

- Nen dung sandwich electrode cho ban cuoi.
- Co the dung interdigitated electrode de test nhanh ban dau.

Cau hinh mua 6 tam Velostat la du rong rai:

- Neu patch 45 x 45 mm: 1 tam cat duoc 36 patch, 6 tam cat duoc 216 patch.
- Voi ma tran 5 x 9 = 45 cell, 6 tam du lam nhieu ban thu va du phong rat nhieu.
- Co the danh rieng 1 tam de test keo, dien cuc, dung cu cat, va hieu chuan.

## Khuyen nghi kich thuoc patch

Voi cell 98 x 98 mm:

- Tiet kiem: 40 x 40 mm.
- Can bang: 45 x 45 mm.
- Tin hieu manh hon: 50 x 50 mm.

Vi ban dat 6 tam, nen dung 45 x 45 mm hoac 50 x 50 mm, khong can ep xuong 40 x 40 mm nua.

## Khuyen nghi day va doc tin hieu

### Cach 1 - Doc tung cell doc lap

Moi sensor co 2 cuc. Co the noi mot cuc ve GND chung, cuc con lai vao bo chia ap va analog multiplexer.

Voi 45 cell:

- Dung 3 module CD74HC4067, moi module 16 kenh, tong 48 kenh.
- Moi kenh doc mot sensor.
- De hieu chuan, it bi ghosting hon ma tran hang-cot.

Khuyen nghi cho giai do R&D.

### Cach 2 - Ma tran hang-cot

Voi 5 x 9 cell:

- 5 day cot + 9 day hang = 14 day.
- Moi giao diem la mot sensor Velostat.

Uu diem la giam day rat manh. Nhuoc diem la co ghosting/crosstalk khi nhieu diem bi ep cung luc, trong khi nguoi nam se ep nhieu diem cung luc. Phai scan va hieu chuan ky.

Khuyen nghi chi dung neu ban da quen xu ly ma tran cam bien.

## Luu y co khi va an toan

- Khong de dong tran cham truc tiep vao da.
- Tat ca dien cuc phai nam duoi lop vai/TPU mong.
- Meo copper tape phai duoc bo tron hoac phu bang bang keo/vai de khong cat vao silicone.
- Can co strain relief tai diem han day: dung keo silicone, epoxy deo, hot glue hoac bang Kapton.
- Day nen di trong khe giua cac cell, khong di tren dinh cell.
- Khong nen dung tam dong lon cung, vi se lam mat do mem cua dem va tao diem can.

## Phuong an chot de che tao mau dau

- Ma tran: 5 x 9.
- Velostat patch: 50 x 50 mm neu khong qua lo chi phi; 45 x 45 mm neu muon can bang.
- Dien cuc: sandwich electrode neu co thoi gian lam can than.
- Vat lieu dien cuc: copper tape mong hoac vai dan dien.
- Doc tin hieu: tung cell qua analog multiplexer, khong dung ma tran hang-cot o ban dau.
