# Tinh so tam Velostat 28 x 28 cm can mua

Ngay cap nhat: 2026-07-24

## Dau vao

- Kich thuoc 1 tam Velostat ban san: 28 x 28 cm = 280 x 280 mm.
- Cell khi hien tai: 98 x 98 mm.
- Nen cat Velostat thanh patch rieng tung cell, khong trai mot tam lon lien tuc.
- So tam can mua = ceil(so cell / so patch cat duoc tren 1 tam).

## So patch cat duoc tu 1 tam 280 x 280 mm

| Kich thuoc patch | Cach cat tren 1 tam | So patch / tam | Ghi chu |
|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 40 x 40 mm | 7 x 7 | 49 | Tiet kiem nhat, gan nhu khong du mep du |
| 45 x 45 mm | 6 x 6 | 36 | Can bang, con du 10 mm moi chieu |
| 50 x 50 mm | 5 x 5 | 25 | Tin hieu manh hon, ton vat lieu hon |
| 60 x 60 mm | 4 x 4 | 16 | Khong khuyen nghi neu can tiet kiem |
| 90 x 90 mm | 3 x 3 | 9 | Gan phu day cell, rat ton Velostat |

## Bang so tam can mua theo ma tran

| Ma tran | So cell | Patch 40x40 | Patch 45x45 | Patch 50x50 | Patch 90x90 |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 4 x 8 | 32 | 1 tam | 1 tam | 2 tam | 4 tam |
| 5 x 8 | 40 | 1 tam | 2 tam | 2 tam | 5 tam |
| 5 x 9 | 45 | 1 tam | 2 tam | 2 tam | 5 tam |
| 5 x 10 | 50 | 2 tam | 2 tam | 2 tam | 6 tam |
| 6 x 10 | 60 | 2 tam | 2 tam | 3 tam | 7 tam |
| 8 x 8 | 64 | 2 tam | 2 tam | 3 tam | 8 tam |

## Khuyen nghi mua

### Neu chon ma tran 5 x 9

- Neu tiet kiem toi da: mua 1 tam, cat 45 patch kich thuoc 40 x 40 mm.
- Neu muon an toan va co du phong: mua 2 tam, cat patch 45 x 45 mm hoac 50 x 50 mm.

Khuyen nghi thuc te: mua 2 tam.

Ly do:

1. Patch 40 x 40 mm tu 1 tam khong co nhieu mep du, cat loi la thieu.
2. Can Velostat du phong de test dien cuc, keo dan, may do ADC.
3. Phi ship cao, mua thieu roi dat lai se ton hon.
4. 2 tam van du cho 5 x 9 hoac 5 x 10 neu dung patch 45 x 45 hoac 50 x 50.

### Neu van giu 8 x 8

- Patch 40 x 40 mm: can 2 tam.
- Patch 45 x 45 mm: can 2 tam.
- Patch 50 x 50 mm: can 3 tam.
- Patch 90 x 90 mm: can 8 tam.

Khong khuyen nghi 8 x 8 vi no lang phi be ngang va tang so cell/valve/cam bien.

## Ket luan chot

Neu theo cau hinh minh khuyen nghi:

- Ma tran: 5 x 9 = 45 cell.
- Patch Velostat: 45 x 45 mm hoac 50 x 50 mm.
- So tam nen mua: 2 tam 28 x 28 cm.

Neu ngan sach rat chat:

- Ma tran: 5 x 9 = 45 cell.
- Patch Velostat: 40 x 40 mm.
- So tam toi thieu: 1 tam 28 x 28 cm.

Neu can quyet dinh mua hang ngay: mua 2 tam.
