# Khuyen nghi mua Velostat va ti le ma tran dem khi

Ngay cap nhat: 2026-07-24

## Ket luan nhanh

Khong nen lam ban full-body ngay o giai do hien tai. Nen lam module hinh chu nhat tu vung vai/scapula den vung mong-dui tren.

Phuong an khuyen nghi cho nguoi truong thanh binh thuong:

- Ma tran cell: 5 x 9, trong do 5 cell theo ngang vai/hong, 9 cell theo doc than nguoi.
- Kich thuoc cell hien tai: 98 x 98 mm.
- Neu de khe lap rap 2 mm giua cac cell, pitch xap xi 100 mm.
- Kich thuoc module xap xi: 500 x 900 mm.
- So cell / tui khi / valve: 45.
- Velostat: cat patch rieng tung cell, khong trai mot tam lien tuc.
- Kich thuoc patch nen dung: 40 x 40 mm hoac 45 x 45 mm.
- So tam Velostat 28 x 28 cm nen mua: 2 tam la hop ly. Neu cuc ky tiet kiem co the dung 1 tam voi patch 40 x 40 mm, nhung it du phong.

## Vi sao khong nen dung 8 x 8

Voi cell 98 x 98 mm:

- 8 x 8 tao ra module xap xi 784 x 784 mm neu khong tinh khe ho.
- Hinh vuong nay thua be ngang nhung lai khong toi uu theo chieu dai co the.
- Co the tiet kiem cell, valve, ong khi, dau doc cam bien neu chuyen sang hinh chu nhat.

So sanh nhanh:

| Ma tran | So cell | Kich thuoc chua tinh khe | Nhan xet |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| 8 x 8 | 64 | 784 x 784 mm | Vuong, lang phi be ngang, khong toi uu theo than nguoi |
| 4 x 8 | 32 | 392 x 784 mm | Rat tiet kiem nhung hoi hep cho vai/hong nguoi lon |
| 5 x 8 | 40 | 490 x 784 mm | Ngang on, doc hoi ngan neu tinh tu vai den dui |
| 5 x 9 | 45 | 490 x 882 mm | Khuyen nghi: can bang giua do phu va chi phi |
| 5 x 10 | 50 | 490 x 980 mm | Phu tot hon den dui/knee, tang them 5 valve/cell |
| 6 x 10 | 60 | 588 x 980 mm | Rong rai nhung gan bang chi phi 8 x 8 |

## Vung co nen phu trong ban dau

Nen phu:

1. Vai / xuong ba vai.
2. Lung tren-lung giua.
3. Lung duoi.
4. Cung cut / mong / vung ischial.
5. Dui tren.

Chua can phu full-body ngay vi:

- Dau va canh tay khong phai vung dieu khien chinh cua dem khi.
- Chan duoi thuong tao ap luc it hon so voi mong/cung cut, tru vung got chan.
- Lam full-body se tang manh so valve, ong khi, day cam bien va loi lap rap.

Luu y: got chan van la vung nguy co loet ti de quan trong. Neu muon tuyen bo phong ngua toan dien tren giuong, nen them module got chan rieng ve sau, vi du 4 x 3 cell hoac 4 x 4 cell dat o vung calf/heel. Khong nen ep module vai-mong va module got chan vao cung mot ma tran ngay tu dau.

## Cat Velostat tu tam 28 x 28 cm

Kich thuoc mot tam: 280 x 280 mm.

| Kich thuoc patch | So patch toi da tren 1 tam | Ghi chu |
|---:|---:|---|
| 40 x 40 mm | 7 x 7 = 49 patch | Du cho 5 x 9 = 45 cell, con 4 patch du phong |
| 45 x 45 mm | 6 x 6 = 36 patch | Tin hieu tot hon 40 mm, can 2 tam cho 5 x 9 |
| 50 x 50 mm | 5 x 5 = 25 patch | Tin hieu tot, nhung ton vat lieu hon |

Khuyen nghi thuc te:

- Neu uu tien tiet kiem toi da: 1 tam, patch 40 x 40 mm, ma tran toi da 5 x 9.
- Neu phi ship cao va muon tranh phai dat lai: 2 tam, patch 45 x 45 mm cho 5 x 9; hoac patch 50 x 50 mm cho 5 x 9/5 x 10.
- Khong nen mua 3 tam neu chua quay lai 8 x 8 hoac chua lam full-body.

## Cach dat patch tren tung cell

Khong nen trai Velostat thanh mot tam lon tren toan bo dem, vi no se noi cau giua cac cell va lam mo ban do ap luc.

Nen dung moi cell mot patch doc lap:

- Patch nam tren dinh tui silicone/lop vai tiep xuc gan benh nhan.
- Patch dat gan tam cell.
- Day dien di trong khe giua cac cell.
- Patch duoc giu long o 4 goc de co the uon theo tui khi khi phong/xep.

Voi cell 98 x 98 mm:

- Patch 40 x 40 mm: tiet kiem, du cho ban do ap luc tuong doi.
- Patch 45 x 45 mm: can bang tot nhat.
- Patch 50 x 50 mm: manh tin hieu hon nhung can nhieu Velostat hon.

## Khuyen nghi chon ngay

Chon cau hinh:

- Cell matrix: 5 x 9.
- Velostat patch: 45 x 45 mm neu mua 2 tam; 40 x 40 mm neu chi mua 1 tam.
- So tam nen dat: 2 tam 28 x 28 cm.

Day la diem can bang hop ly giua:

- Do phu co the nguoi lon.
- Giam lang phi so voi 8 x 8.
- Giam so valve/ong khi/cam bien.
- Van du du lieu de huan luyen/kiem thu thuat toan ap suat-thoi gian.
